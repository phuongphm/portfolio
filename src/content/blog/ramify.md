## It started with a personal struggle
Have you ever looked at an IELTS Writing Task 2 question and struggled
to come up with ideas? I certainly have.

One of my biggest challenges was dealing with unfamiliar social topics.
I often found it difficult to generate arguments, explore different
perspectives, or develop convincing examples due to my limited
background knowledge.

Over time, I discovered that mind mapping was one of the most effective
learning methods for me. It helped me connect ideas, develop logical
thinking, and remember information more effectively. However, creating
mind maps manually was often time-consuming.

That made me wonder: **What if AI could help me turn ideas into
structured mind maps?**

## The birth of Ramify

That question became the starting point of **Ramify IELTS**, a personal
project inspired by my own learning experience.

The idea was simple: learners enter an IELTS Writing Task 2 question,
and AI generates a visual mind map containing main ideas, supporting
points, relevant examples, and topic-related keywords.

Rather than simply generating a complete essay, I wanted Ramify to help
learners explore a topic, organize their thoughts, and build a logical
foundation before writing.

## Turning an idea into reality

I developed Ramify using Google AI Studio and worked on the project to
participate in **AI Riser Vietnam**.

During development, I encountered several challenges. AI-generated
responses were sometimes too lengthy, repetitive, or difficult to
organize into clear mind map branches. Main arguments, supporting ideas,
and examples were not always separated logically.

To improve the results, I experimented with different prompts, tested
the outputs, and continuously refined my instructions until the
generated content became more concise and structured.

Although I did not win the competition, participating in AI Riser
Vietnam gave me a valuable opportunity to experiment with AI and turn a
personal idea into a working project.

## What Ramify taught me

This project taught me that technology does not always need to reinvent
the way we learn. Sometimes, it simply needs to make effective learning
methods more accessible.

Mind mapping was already a useful strategy for me. Through Ramify, I
explored how AI could make this process faster and more convenient
without taking away the thinking process itself.

What began as a personal challenge in learning IELTS gradually became an
opportunity to explore the intersection of AI and education.

For me, Ramify is more than just an AI-powered learning tool. It is a
reminder that meaningful ideas can grow from the challenges we
experience ourselves.
